profile

My path into product was not a straight line. After a degree in Finance and Banking, I started in business development, working on sales, pricing and inventory. The work ran on numbers, yet I was always more curious about why a customer chose one product over another. That curiosity led me into the world of user experience and product. Finance built the habit of reading numbers, UX built the habit of reading people, and product work needs both.

From 2021 to 2026, I worked at Mobile World Group (MWG), Vietnam's largest retail group by revenue. I started on the website of Thế Giới Di Động, MWG's electronics retail chain, including the Erablue Indonesia project in 2022. In 2024, I moved to the web and app platform of Nhà thuốc An Khang, MWG's pharmacy chain, working on pre-order, An Khang v4.0, report automation and other projects. Across both, my work covered product strategy, UX design, data analytics and workflow automation.

profile

My path into product was not a straight line. After a degree in Finance and Banking, my early career was in business: managing product categories, forecasting sales, working out margins. Over time my interest moved elsewhere: understanding why users do what they do, then designing so it gets easier. Finance built the habit of reading numbers, UX built the habit of reading people, and product work needs both.

From 2021 to 2024, I worked on the Thế Giới Di Động website, including the Erablue Indonesia project in 2022. From 2024, the focus moved to the Nhà thuốc An Khang web and app platform: pre-order, An Khang v4.0 and data reporting. The role covered product strategy, experience design and analytics.
